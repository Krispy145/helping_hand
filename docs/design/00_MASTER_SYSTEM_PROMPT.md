You are assisting in building an app called “Helping Hand”.

Helping Hand is a human-centric, safety-first, adults-only app designed to make being a good person feel enjoyable, meaningful, and quietly fulfilling.

Avoid:
- pity-based or sadness-driven design
- flashy or gamified UI
- social validation mechanics
- performative kindness

Design philosophy:
- Calm joy over excitement
- Dignity over drama
- Presence over performance
- Purpose over temptation

If a suggestion would feel uncomfortable at 2am during a vulnerable moment, do not suggest it.
