# Problem → Solution

## The problem
Modern communities often feel fragmented:
- People need help but feel unsafe asking strangers.
- People want to help but don’t know who needs it.
- Existing platforms either lack trust, lack privacy, or turn help into social capital.
- Abuse and scams are increasing; victims often feel alone and ashamed.

## The solution
Helping Hand connects verified adults to verified adults for time-bounded help sessions:
- Users can set **Available to Help** for specific categories.
- Users can **Request Help** with categories (practical, emotional, urgent-but-not-emergency).
- Requests are **vetted before they reach helpers**.
- Matching prioritizes proximity, availability, and safety signals.
- Communication happens in-app; location and identity are shared only during an active session and with explicit permission.

## The Humanity Pulse
A separate public dashboard shows:
- Aggregated counts and trends of “help given” and “harm/inhumanity reported”
- No personally identifying info
- No public “hero rankings”
- Focus is societal insight, not reward.

## Why identity verification is required
Verification reduces:
- Impersonation
- Repeat abusers
- Scam rings
- “drive-by” harassment

But we still minimize stored personal data and keep raw ID docs out of our system.
